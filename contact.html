COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You Contact Us

Some useful information to help you prepare your enquiry.

Send an Enquiry
What information should I provide?

Share the type of service, a short description of the issue, your property location and a suitable way to contact you.

Can I request a price estimate first?

Yes. Describe the work and ask for an estimate. The final amount may depend on the site, labour, materials and scope.

Can I request service for a business property?

Yes. Select the commercial service option and include details about your office, shop or other property.

Does submitting this form confirm a booking?

No. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Availability, charges and scheduling need to be confirmed with the service provider.
