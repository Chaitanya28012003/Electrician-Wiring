GOOD TO KNOW

Pricing Questions

Here are a few common questions to help you understand how an electrical service estimate may be prepared.

What determines the final price?

The final estimate may depend on the type of work, time required, materials, site conditions and travel requirements.

Are materials included in the listed prices?

Not necessarily. Material costs should be confirmed in your individual quotation before the work begins.

Can I get a custom quote for my property?

Yes. Share the type of service and basic project details through the contact page to request an estimate.

Do you offer business maintenance packages?

You can enquire about a package tailored to the property, expected work and preferred maintenance schedule.

When should I confirm the final price?

Confirm the scope, labour, materials and any additional charges with the service provider before approving the job.
