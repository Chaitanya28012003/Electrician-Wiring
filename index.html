Small Warning Signs
Deserve Attention.

Electrical concerns should be taken seriously. If something seems unusual, stop using the affected equipment when safe to do so and seek qualified assistance.

Repeated circuit breaker trips
Unusual heat or burning smells
Flickering lights or recurring power issues
Damaged outlets, switches or visible wiring
